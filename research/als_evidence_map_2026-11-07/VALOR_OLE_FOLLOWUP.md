## Design and denominators

Original randomization retained; all OLE participants received tofersen. OLE study personnel, participants, and caregivers remained blinded to original VALOR assignment. No concurrent untreated OLE control. OLE completion counts are not observed-case denominators at week 148.

Population: Adults with SOD1-ALS. Comparison: Early start versus tofersen started approximately six months later.

Randomized early/delayed: 72/36; entered OLE: 95; completed early/delayed: 34/12; tofersen-exposed safety population: 104.

Trials: NCT02623699, NCT03070119.

[valor_ole_final_2025](https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jamaneurology/fullarticle/2843130) (Methods: Trial Design; Results: Participants; Safety)

## Methods and limitations

- **SAP timing:** Integration and NfL subgrouping were described as prespecified before initial VALOR database lock. Full-ITT NfL-adjusted testing was added after original analyses. Integrated SAP versions: June 8, 2021 to September 12, 2024; not the standalone OLE protocol. [valor_ole_supplement_1](https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jamaneurology/fullarticle/2843130) (eMethods, PDF pages 6-8)
- **Subgroup identity:** NfL-defined subgroups differ from the original primary population defined using variant and prerandomization slope. Do not equate them. [valor_ole_supplement_1](https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jamaneurology/fullarticle/2843130) (eTable 3, PDF page 16)
- **Missing data and timepoint:** Week 148 used assessment availability (at least 40% across combined groups) and scheduling. Multiple imputation and reference-based sensitivity analyses were used. Attrition remains important. [valor_ole_supplement_1](https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jamaneurology/fullarticle/2843130) (eMethods, PDF pages 8-10)
- **Survival precision:** The supplement warns that estimates after week 192 are unreliable because numbers at risk fall. Do not interpret subgroup median differences as a precise causal survival extension. [valor_ole_supplement_1](https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jamaneurology/fullarticle/2843130) (eMethods, PDF page 11)
- **Interpretation:** Small sample, crossover and limited power constrain conclusions. Follow-up does not replace the original randomized primary analysis or establish benefit for all ALS. [valor_ole_final_2025](https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jamaneurology/fullarticle/2843130) (Limitations)
- **Source anomalies excluded:** The publisher Table 2 has an impossible negative upper confidence limit for slower-subgroup death; Figure 2 has nonmonotone ITT delayed-group risk counts. These entries were not extracted or silently repaired. They leave source-level uncertainty beyond the selected concordant estimates. [valor_ole_final_2025](https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jamaneurology/fullarticle/2843130) (Table 2: slower-subgroup death; Figure 2: ITT risk table (weeks 156 and 182))
- **Sponsor role:** Biogen funded the study and participated in design, data management, analysis, interpretation and report preparation. Peer review does not remove these potential interests. [valor_ole_final_2025](https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jamaneurology/fullarticle/2843130) (Article Information: Funding/Support and Role of the Funder/Sponsor)
