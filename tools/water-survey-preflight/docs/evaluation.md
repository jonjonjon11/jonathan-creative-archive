# Small, honest evaluation plan

## Hypothesis, not finding

Some survey teams may find a no-account, offline, row-level review useful before handing data to a central uploader. WPdx already validates uploads. No practitioner interview, comparative workflow test, field deployment, or outcome study has established demand or added value here.

## Smallest useful test

1. Ask two consenting water-data practitioners whether this problem exists in their actual workflow, before proposing wider adoption. Do not upload their records or request identifying information.
2. Have them use the supplied synthetic fixtures. Record only voluntarily provided, non-sensitive observations: whether the flagged issues are understandable, whether a warning is mistaken for water-safety advice, and where a normal workflow already solves the same problem.
3. Compare against their existing offline/spreadsheet and WPdx upload review approaches. Measure task time and correction accuracy on an agreed synthetic fixture, not download counts or compliments.
4. Stop or revise if the tool adds redundant work, encourages guessing missing observations, confuses water presence with safety, or misses common spreadsheet exports. Preserve counterexamples and report them rather than advertising a success.

Suggested early continuation bar: both practitioners identify a concrete pre-upload gap and can complete the synthetic exercise without safety/certification misconceptions. This is a proposed decision rule, not evidence of effectiveness or a validated sample size.

No outreach, real-data collection, deployment, or ongoing service was performed as part of this prototype build. Further testing needs a named workflow owner, appropriate consent, and a clear policy for handling location and personal information.
