# Sources and provenance

Checked **2026-11-07 UTC** by opening the current official pages and following the standard link. Dates below distinguish document dates from retrieval dates. Search-engine freshness labels were not treated as publication dates.

1. **Current share-data landing page:** https://www.waterpointdata.org/share-data/ . Its “Download WPdx Data Standard PDF” link pointed to the January 2021 PDF below. This observation does not assert that a newer standard cannot exist elsewhere.
2. **WPdx Data Standard, updated January 2021:** https://www.waterpointdata.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/WPDx_Data_Standard.pdf . Read all three pages. Basis for required fields, the source/technology alternative, open text, coordinate guidance, binary responses, and optional years.
3. **WPdx MEAL annex, published December 2023:** https://www.waterpointdata.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/WPdx_MEAL_Guide_Dec2023.pdf . Read the introduction and required-parameter tables, PDF pages 3–7. The PDF retains a March 2022 working-user-guide front matter; the annex's publication context comes from the dated announcement below. It recommends consistent responses while retaining open text and describes the risk of cleaning misinterpretation.
4. **Dated MEAL announcement, 2023-12-07:** https://www.waterpointdata.org/2023/12/07/announcing-the-wpdx-monitoring-evaluation-adapting-and-learning-meal-guide/ . Confirms use before upload and the role of human interpretation in cleaning.
5. **Current access-data page:** https://www.waterpointdata.org/access-data/ . Its WPdx-Basic description already covers completeness, country-boundary checks, and normalization. The prototype does not duplicate that entire process.

The code was written for this experiment. Public reference facts informed a conservative, documented subset of rules. No third-party implementation, field dataset, survey submission, downloaded PDF, private record, or restricted material is included. `fixtures/` contains invented observations, not real field findings. No external organization was contacted for this experiment.
