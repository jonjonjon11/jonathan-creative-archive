02 / Confirm the meaning

Map your columns

Exact WPdx field names are suggested, with or without #. For other headers, choose the matching column yourself. A column cannot serve two fields. Changes require another review.

Source and technology are open text, not a fixed list. Optional columns not listed here, geographic boundaries, and the truth of survey observations are not validated.

Error: missing required information, an impossible coordinate, or input this checker cannot interpret. Warning: a human review question, not proof of incorrect data. These are this prototype’s severity levels, not WPdx rejection decisions.

Reports default to omitting input headers and values. They are not anonymous: review before sharing. The report contains findings, not a corrected dataset.

What remains: verify WGS 84 coordinates and country boundaries, confirm each observation and the meaning of unknowns with the data collector, review data-sharing rights, and use the official WPdx process if submitting. No findings does not mean complete, accurate, safe, or accepted.

Keep people in the loop.

Never fill a gap by guessing. Do not turn a water-presence response into a safety claim. Duplicate observations can be legitimate visits; this tool never deletes or fixes records.

Close or clear the tab when finished. No cookies, analytics, local storage, or remote services are used. Downloaded reports and browser-managed file history remain on your device.
