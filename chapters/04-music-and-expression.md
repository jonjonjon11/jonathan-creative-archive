The technical connection between matter and music is an explicitly chosen mapping. Rest-energy/Compton frequency, de Broglie wavelength, transition frequency, Larmor frequency, musical cents, and assigned audible tones are different quantities. Elements do not each have one intrinsic musical note. [SRC-016]

## Two distinct uses of sound

**Creative expression:** play, composition, rhythmic interaction, instruments, collaborative performance, and emotionally meaningful spaces.

**Data representation:** a chosen mapping from model values to audible features, such as pitch, rhythm, intensity, or timbre. Such mappings can support exploration, but they are design choices. An audible analogy does not mean an astronomical or quantum process literally makes that sound in the depicted conditions.

These uses can coexist without being confused. A “creative mode” can invite expressive transformations, while an “inspect mode” explains exactly what controls the audio.

## A proposed first sound experiment

This is a new build suggestion, not a recovered implementation.

Use the same simple oscillator as a visual lesson. Map phase to a visual marker and optionally map a bounded model value to an audible parameter. Show the mapping, units of the model, and output bounds. Let the learner change amplitude, frequency, and playback speed while seeing which operations affect the model and which affect presentation.

Acceptance criteria:

- Sound is optional and starts only after explicit interaction.
- A stop/mute control is always available.
- A text and visual equivalent conveys the same lesson.
- Output is bounded and avoids sudden excessive changes.
- A saved experiment records the mapping, not only an audio file.
- Content rights are documented for every sample, score, or recording.
- The system does not claim a health effect or therapeutic outcome.

## Collaboration without premature complexity

A shared musical room might someday support call-and-response, rhythm games, co-composition, or movement-linked instruments. First test the activity locally and asynchronously. Network latency, moderation, consent to recording, accessibility, and rights to contributions are distinct problems, not details automatically solved by multiplayer support.

## Unrecovered material

The reviewed material is insufficient to reconstruct the full history of musical ideas or performances. Future additions should distinguish original user-authored work, collaboratively produced work, generated material, references, and licensed assets. If the originating artifact cannot be recovered or its rights are unclear, preserve a factual description and the gap rather than inventing a replacement and calling it historical.
