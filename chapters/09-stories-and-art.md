## Imagine, calculate, question

A recovered three-helium-atom research story narrows a speculative sensory premise into a tractable calculation. It asks how changes of shape might be inferred, how ordinary fluctuations can imitate exotic explanations, and how detecting a deformation differs from identifying its cause. Its historical computational checks were not rerun here. [SRC-019]

This is an educational thought experiment, not an experimentally validated sensor, a quantum-gravity discovery, or a clinical device. It illustrates a useful creative method: let imagination generate a question, make the assumptions explicit, calculate what can actually be tested, then revise the story around the limits.

## Preserve corrections

The recovered quantum handbook contains a formula that a later master document explicitly supersedes. Future salvage should preserve the correction and independently verify the mathematics rather than restoring an older formula because it appears in a polished handbook. [SRC-019]

Stories and beautiful images can make a subject memorable. Their status should remain visible: fiction, authored metaphor, conventional model, sourced data, or speculative claim.
