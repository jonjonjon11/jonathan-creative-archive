## Different upstream licenses

Reviewed source families use different terms. The educational-universe repository reports CC0-1.0. AR Buddy Commons separates AGPL-3.0-or-later source from CC-BY-SA-4.0 documentation/prompts/curricula and a future CERN-OHL-S-2.0 hardware direction. Other repositories have mixed or absent root licensing. [SRC-007, SRC-008, SRC-009]

These are observations from the reviewed records, not a legal audit. File-level notices, dependency licenses, asset-store agreements, authorship, generated materials, and inherited history may introduce additional obligations. A root license does not prove that every bundled file can be redistributed under it.

This archive supplies fresh summaries and minimal quotations for provenance. It includes an unchanged Reality Atlas HTML artifact, a bounded cooperation/rescue authoring kit, and Lumen Commons source plus its reproducible standalone build, each with separately verified CC0 source provenance and complete license text. It does not bulk-copy upstream source trees, asset packs, artwork, audio, or full design documents. If excerpts are later expanded, review their rights and required attribution before inclusion.

## Material excluded from this edition

- Credentials, signing keys, keystores, tokens, or account access material.
- Full Git history from existing projects.
- Third-party art, music, fonts, asset-store packages, or datasets with unverified redistribution rights.
- Personal medical, financial, family, location, or contact details.
- Private conversation transcripts and exact private document/file locators.
- Real patient, child, or participant records.

A repository inspection found credentials-related files and substantial third-party assets in at least one source project. That is a reason to review sources conservatively, not to copy them and rely on a later cleanup. [SRC-009]

## Public discoverability

This public edition includes readable Markdown, explicit licenses, stable concept IDs, JSON metadata, and source summaries to make the material easier to find and interpret. The archive remains selective, with verification limits stated alongside its contents.

`llms.txt` is an optional plain-text navigation aid. It does not guarantee indexing, inclusion in AI training, or uptake by other systems. Public access does not override licenses, privacy, or terms of use.

## Private provenance

This edition refers to source IDs. Exact private conversation and document locators are maintained separately for the review. Public readers may therefore be unable to inspect the original source. That limitation must remain disclosed. If a source later becomes public, add a verified public locator without leaking its former private access details.
