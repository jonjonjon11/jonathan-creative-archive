# Outside-Workspace Actions You Can Do Tonight (No new reports needed)

## Objective
Secure commitments, not just interest.

## 60-minute external sprint

## 1) Contact 5 high-leverage people (20 min)
Use `templates/outreach_message.txt` and send to:
- 1 municipal/public service leader
- 1 school/education leader
- 1 health/community care leader
- 1 civil society/community organizer
- 1 technical/data operations person

Ask for exactly:
- A 30-minute call within 72 hours
- One named delegate
- One concrete contribution for first 100 days

## 2) Schedule one kickoff call (10 min)
Use `templates/kickoff_agenda.md` with:
- Decision rights
- First pilot scope
- Safeguards
- Weekly reporting cadence

## 3) Request one tangible contribution per contact (20 min)
Examples:
- Venue hours for a pilot hub
- Volunteer coordinator
- Existing training room access
- Pro bono legal/privacy review
- Translation support

## 4) Lock follow-through (10 min)
For each contact, log:
- commitment
- owner
- due date
in `ops/stakeholder_list.csv`.

## Non-negotiable tonight outputs
- 5 outbound asks sent
- 1 meeting booked
- 3 concrete resource offers requested
- every commitment logged with an owner/date
