# Kickoff Agenda (30 minutes)

## 1. Mission and constraints (5 min)
- Protect life and dignity first
- No coercive practices
- Transparent commitments and outcomes

## 2. Pilot scope (7 min)
- Geography
- Population served
- 3 services for first 30 days

## 3. Ownership and decision rights (6 min)
- Who decides what
- Escalation path
- Weekly cadence

## 4. Safeguards (5 min)
- Child/vulnerable adult protection
- Data/privacy handling
- Grievance channel

## 5. Metrics and first milestones (5 min)
- Day-30 outcomes
- Day-100 outcomes

## 6. Commitments before close (2 min)
- Named owners
- Due dates
